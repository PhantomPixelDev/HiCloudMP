Features

Everything you need.
Nothing you don't.

Your library, organized

Add your folders once. Browse by name, artist and album, search as you type, and filter the queue without waiting.

Cloud without the sync

Stream from Nextcloud or any WebDAV server, a Subsonic-compatible server, or Ampache. Albums scan in the background; tracks play directly.

A real 10-band EQ

Presets or your own curve, applied live to local and streamed audio. Flat settings are a bit-exact bypass.

A queue that behaves

Title, artist and album columns, drag to reorder, Play Next, sort and filter — all remembered between sessions.

Your phone is the remote

Turn on the web remote and open the link from the same Wi-Fi. No app to install, token protected, local unless you allow it.

Private by default

No account, no telemetry. Settings and playlists stay on your PC; saved cloud passwords are encrypted with Windows DPAPI.

A closer look

Small app. Serious sound.

Tune the room, not just the volume.

Ten bands from 31 Hz to 16 kHz, with presets for rock, bass, vocals and more. Move a slider and hear the change immediately.

Good to know

Small print, plainly.

Where does my data live?

Settings, playlists and cloud accounts live in %LOCALAPPDATA%\HiCloudMP\HiCloudMP. Installing, upgrading or uninstalling never deletes them.

Does it phone home?

No telemetry and no account. The app only talks to the servers you add, and to GitHub when you ask it to check for updates.

How do I uninstall it?

Settings → Apps → HiCloud MP. Your music and settings stay exactly where they are.

What about Linux?

The source runs on Linux with MPRIS media keys; packaged builds are Windows-only for now.
