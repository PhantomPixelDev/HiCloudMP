Features

Local and cloud in one place

Local library that keeps up

Add folders once. Scanning runs off the UI thread, search is instant, and folder watching rescans when new files appear.

Your own cloud, first-class

Nextcloud or any WebDAV server, Subsonic-compatible servers (Subsonic, Navidrome, Airsonic, Nextcloud Music) and Ampache. Background scans, no sync step.

Playlists and a queue that behaves

Import and export M3U, M3U8, PLS and XSPF. Drag to reorder, Play Next, shuffle, repeat — sort and filter survive restarts.

Light on resources

One native Qt process, about 180 MB at idle in our Linux measurement. No Electron, no Chromium, no background services.

Phone remote

Open a token-protected link on the same Wi-Fi. No app to install, and it stays on your LAN unless you allow other devices.

Your formats, your tags

MP3, FLAC, WAV, OGG, Opus, AAC, M4A, WMA and more through the bundled FFmpeg, with cover art pulled from the files.

Why it's light

Measured, not claimed

HiCloud MP is a native Qt application compiled with Nuitka. It is not an Electron app and it does not ship a browser: one process handles the library, the queue, the audio and the phone remote.

~180 MB resident at idle, measured on the packaged Linux build
1 process no Chromium helper zoo, no background daemons
0 accounts no sign-up, no telemetry, no cloud lock-in
  • Your servers stay your servers — WebDAV, Subsonic and Ampache, not somebody else's catalog.
  • Everything needed is in the download: Qt, the FFmpeg multimedia backend and cover-art FFmpeg.
  • Settings, playlists and history are plain files in the standard per-user folder.

Good to know

No account · no telemetry

Where does my data live?

Settings, playlists and history sit in the standard per-user folder: %LOCALAPPDATA%\HiCloudMP\HiCloudMP on Windows, ~/Library/Application Support/HiCloudMP on macOS, ~/.local/share/HiCloudMP on Linux. Installing, upgrading or uninstalling never deletes them.

Is it an Electron app?

No. HiCloud MP is a native Qt application compiled with Nuitka: one process, no Chromium, no bundled browser. The packaged Linux build measured about 180 MB resident at idle.

Does it phone home?

No telemetry and no account. The app only talks to the servers you add, and to GitHub when you ask it to check for updates.

Which cloud servers work?

Nextcloud and any WebDAV server (ownCloud included), Subsonic-compatible servers such as Navidrome, Airsonic and Nextcloud Music, and Ampache. An app password is fine.

How do I uninstall it?

Windows: Settings → Apps → HiCloud MP. macOS: drag the app to the Trash. Linux: delete the AppImage or the unpacked folder. Your music and settings stay exactly where they are.

Are the macOS and Linux builds first-class?

Yes. Linux ships an AppImage and a tarball; macOS ships native arm64 and x86_64 DMGs. The Mac builds are ad-hoc signed but not notarized yet, so the first launch needs a right-click → Open.
